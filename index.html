Executive Summary

Sagar Lalchand Shah (commonly known as Sagar Shah or Sagar L Shah) is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of SaNiSa Solutions, an educational technology company building adaptive learning, active recall, attention recovery, and AI-assisted assessment systems (including LearnYet, AquaLearn, FocusYou, AssessQ&A, and MentorDesk).

Prior to founding SaNiSa Solutions, he spent over two decades in global banking and telecommunications technology, serving as Vice President at J.P. Morgan & Chase and earlier as a Software Developer at Essar Telecom Limited.

Mentorship, Robotics & Recognition

International Robotics Mentorship — RoboCup Junior Asia-Pacific 2025 (Abu Dhabi): Mentored Team RCJ BotMasters to win the Best Learning Journal Award in the RCJ Rescue Line Primary category (Official Results | News Report).
National Robotics Mentorship — Robotex India 2025: Mentored competitive youth robotics team (including Nirvan Sagar Shah) in the Maze Solver Intermediate category at the National Level Robotex 2025 championship (Highlight Reel).
Web Platforms & Media Contributions: Founder and operator of sanisasolutions.com, learnyet.com, and marriageextra.com. Credited contributor for insights on Indian arranged marriages in the award-winning 2017 documentary A Suitable Girl.
